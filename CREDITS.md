# Monita — Origins and Credits

Monita is a self-hosted notification, messaging, and automation project developed and maintained by **Gigabyte Grove**, a technology-connected homestead that builds and shares tools created for real-world needs.

## Upstream foundation

Monita originated as a fork of [Gotify](https://github.com/gotify/server), an MIT-licensed open-source notification server originally developed by jmattheis and other contributors. We acknowledge and appreciate that foundation and the work of its contributors.

Monita has since undergone extensive independent development, including its current channels, messaging, collaboration, integration, administration, and automation direction. Monita is **independently maintained**; it is not an official Gotify release and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Gotify developers. Issue reports concerning Monita should be filed with [Gigabyte Grove](https://github.com/gigabytegrove/monita/issues), not the upstream project.

This acknowledgment does not imply that every present-day Monita component originated upstream, nor that upstream contributors are responsible for Gigabyte Grove changes.

## Software licenses and copyright

Gigabyte Grove's independently authored modifications and additions should be identified as its contributions without deleting the notices applicable to inherited code.
